Why now

AI risk is escalating from "content risk" to "action risk"

Traditional generative AI mostly outputs text. Agents plan, execute multi-step tasks, and connect to internal data, browsers, databases, code environments, MCP/tools and external systems. Once they hold payment, approval, record-modification or device-control authority, a single indirect prompt injection or permission breach can trigger catastrophic financial loss, data leakage, or physical damage.

⚖️

Regulation is shifting

NIST and the UK AI Security Institute are moving focus from model safety to agent identity, authorization, and controllable action.

🔓

Trust gaps are structural

Most enterprises have no AI red team of their own — and a vendor grading its own agent's safety is an inherent conflict of interest.

🔄

One-time testing isn't enough

Models, prompts, knowledge bases, tools and attacker capability all keep changing after go-live — a single pre-launch test can't cover it.

The platform

One security loop across the entire agent lifecycle

Sentinel isn't three products sold separately — it's a single closed loop. Assurance is both revenue and the entry point that captures real attack data, business boundaries and workflows; Guard turns that into high-frequency daily use and recurring revenue; Continuous Assurance keeps every safety conclusion current.
