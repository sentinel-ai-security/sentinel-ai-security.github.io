Company

Building the default independent safety layer for AI agents

Here's how we think about the market, and how we're going after it.

Why this, why now

The investment thesis

📈

Structural demand

Agents are moving from generating content to executing action. Enterprise safety controls have to keep pace.

🚪

A clear wedge

Enterprise buyers lack their own AI red team, and a vendor grading its own agent is a conflict of interest. Independent verification has a natural buyer.

🔁

A path to recurring revenue

Assurance is the entry point to Guard and Continuous Assurance, turning one-time project revenue into ongoing subscription.

🧠

A compounding moat

Every engagement strengthens the next. See the flywheel on our product page.

Go-to-market

Land and expand

We land with a high-touch Assurance engagement on a high-risk, permission-clear agent, then expand into Guard and Continuous Assurance using the threat model and trust built along the way.

Land

Enterprises actively deploying high-authority agents, starting in finance, procurement and internal tooling.

Expand

Assurance findings become the basis for Guard and Continuous Assurance, converting project revenue into subscription.

Team-led sales

Founders drive early customer discovery and technical delivery, building toward a repeatable sales motion.

Business model

From project revenue to safety infrastructure

Stage What the customer buys Pricing logic
1. First assurance Sentinel Assurance Per project / per system
2. Go-live Sentinel Guard Enterprise subscription + usage
3. System change Continuous Assurance Re-testing & annual service
4. Ecosystem Rating / standards / audit interfaces Enterprise contracts, partnerships

This business model, along with our product roadmap and milestones, reflects current startup hypotheses under active validation with customers, engineering practice, and standards bodies. It should not be read as fully realized product capability.
