Sentinel Fellowship

A research fellowship in AI agent security

The Sentinel Fellowship supports researchers working on open problems in AI agent security. Fellows spend six months on one research project, mentored by our founders, with the goal of a public result: a paper, an open-source tool, or a benchmark.

Research areas

Red-teaming AI agents, runtime guardrails, neuron-level safety and repair, and how to tell when a system has been tested enough to deploy.

What fellows get

A £1,000 stipend for the six months, close mentorship from researchers who publish at top security and ML venues, and support to publish the result. Hybrid.

Who it's for

PhD students, postdocs, and engineers with a research background who want to work on AI security problems that matter in practice.
